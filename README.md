# Tech To Craft LLC

**Bridging the gap between technology and craft — through software, consulting, and books.**

Tech To Craft LLC is an independent software and AI engineering studio. We partner directly with founders and technical leaders to build resilient systems, establish robust quality automation, and deploy production-grade AI solutions with confidence.

---

### What We Do

* **Technical Consulting** — Architecture reviews, technology selection, code audits, roadmap planning, and curated technical workshops.
* **Automated Evals & Guardrails** — Custom evaluation harnesses, regression testing, red-teaming, and latency/accuracy benchmarks for production AI agents and models.
* **Quality & Automation Consulting** — Zero-to-one CI/CD pipelines, quality gates, automated testing strategies, and structured team handovers.

---

### How We Work

1. **Listen & Understand** — Grounding every technical decision in your real goals, team context, and constraints.
2. **Craft & Deliver** — Iterating transparently with clean, maintainable, and resilient engineering.
3. **Support & Empower** — Standing by our work post-delivery with team handovers, documentation, and ongoing guidance.
